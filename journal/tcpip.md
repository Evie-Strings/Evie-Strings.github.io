# The TCP/IP Model

## Applications

Applications is the first level of sending messages over the internet. Messages are in binary format.

Examples of applications:

- www
- email

## Transport

TCP - Reliable, data is correct and complete on arrival

UDP - Fast, not necessarily accurate, useful for live streams and online games

## Internet

Gives each device an IP address and sends packages to their destinations.

## Link

Protocols in this layer include:

- Wifi
- Ethernet
- LTE
- Bluetooth
