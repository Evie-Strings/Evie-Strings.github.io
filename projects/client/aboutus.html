Hello! Thank you so much for visiting Yarn Ahoy! We greatly appreciate your visit and purchase, and any proceeds go to me, Adela, and my great friend who designed this website, Evie. I started crocheting in 2023, and have started to make my own patterns for personal use, make lots of random things, and start a yarn collection. Evie needed a client to create a website for, and she went above and beyond designing this one for me. With my assistance, she was able to create a personalized website for me to continue pursuing my hobby. I was inspired by Scoops Ahoy when creating my website, but was chased away from the design by the moderate threat of getting sued from a certain big red streaming service. Now, Yarn Ahoy is just ocean/nautical themed, and because Evie did nearly all the work, this website is up and running, and I am not being sued for various reasons. Please help me thank her by purchasing today!!
